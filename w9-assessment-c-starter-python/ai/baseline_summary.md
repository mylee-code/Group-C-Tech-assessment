# Human Baseline Summary — Task 1

**Data provenance:** `ai/workshops_spring2026.csv` is a synthetic dataset created for i.c.stars Tech Foundations Performance Assessment C (Week 9), November 2026. The "Neighborhood Tech Hub," its workshops, and every name and email address in the file are fictional.

**Written by:** program staff, numbers checked by hand. Use this as your baseline: the standard you compare the AI's summary against.

---

From March 3 to May 26, 2026, the Neighborhood Tech Hub ran 24 free digital-skills workshops, four in each of six Chicago neighborhoods. 518 people registered and 394 attended, a 76% attendance rate.

Time of day mattered most. Weekday 10 a.m. sessions averaged 55% attendance, compared with 80% for weekday 6 p.m. sessions and 86% for Saturday 11 a.m. sessions.

Austin and Englewood had the lowest overall attendance (68% and 69%), but they also hosted four of the six morning sessions. Outside the morning slot, their attendance (80–81%) matched the other neighborhoods.

Attendees rated the workshops 4.4 out of 5 on average (range 3.9 to 4.9). Online Job Search drove follow-up interest: it produced 41 of the 93 sign-ups for a next course.

**Recommendation:** move weekday morning sessions to evenings or Saturdays before drawing any conclusion about interest in a particular neighborhood.

**Limits:** one season of data; ratings come only from attendees who stayed to the end; we could not check for duplicate registrations.
